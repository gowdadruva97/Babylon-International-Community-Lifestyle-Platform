# Babylon-International-Community-Lifestyle-Platform

This project focuses on developing and growing Babylon, a digital community platform designed to help immigrants, expatriates and international users settle into new countries and build meaningful connections.

The platform brings together essential services, local information and community resources in one place, helping users access accommodation, job opportunities, local events, cultural content and practical guidance when moving to or living in a new country.

Project Overview:

The platform is structured around four key areas:

1. Media & Information: Provides useful articles, lifestyle content, cultural stories, current affairs and practical guides to help users understand their new environment.

2. Community & Marketplace: Enables users to connect with local communities, explore job opportunities, find accommodation and discover goods and services.

3. Events & Engagement: Helps users discover local events, social gatherings, meetups and multicultural experiences to build connections and engage with their communities.

4. Radio & Cultural Content: Provides access to international music, radio shows and diverse cultural stories, helping users stay connected with different cultures and communities.

Key Features:

Centralised platform combining community, marketplace, media and event discovery.
Job and accommodation discovery for people relocating to new countries.
Community groups that encourage networking and information sharing.
Local event discovery and participation.
Practical relocation guides covering housing, legal documentation and integration.
Multicultural radio and editorial content.
International expansion approach, starting with Ireland and extending to other locations.
